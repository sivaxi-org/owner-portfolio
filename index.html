Platform

Ledger — real-time billing engine

Northwind's old billing job ran nightly and reconciliation errors took days to trace. I redesigned it around an event-sourced model: every usage event is appended immutably, balances are derived rather than mutated, and disputes resolve by replaying the log instead of guessing. It now processes 40M+ events a day with zero reconciliation drift since launch.
